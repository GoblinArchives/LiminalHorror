#LIMINAL HORROR PRINT CATALOG
Survival Horror Tabletop Gaming

Liminal Horror ttrpg has hundreds of first and third party digital content. Included below is a catalog featuring all of the physical print products currently available for Liminal Horror. For a full digital catalog of Liminal Horror resources, adventures, and tools, please go to the [Liminal Horror Library](https://liminalhorrorlibrary.com/).

##CORE RULES

### LIMINAL HORROR DELUXE EDITION
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Zach Hazard Vaupen
A hardcover core book featuring rules, procedures for play, facilitator essays, monsters, a setting guide, three scenarios, supplemental rules, resources, and reference aids.
[460 pages, 6" x 9" sewn hardcover, Color cover, color interiors, MSRP $60]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

LIMINAL HORROR INVESTIGATORS EDITION
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Zach Hazard Vaupen
Everything you need to play Liminal Horror. A table-ready version of the core rules for players and facilitators.
[44 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, Color cover, black & white interiors, MSRP $13]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

LIMINAL HORROR - SPANISH LANGUAGE EDITION
Goblin Archives, Mario la esquina del rol, Josh Domanski, Zach Hazard Vaupen
Spanish language translation of Liminal Horror Investigator Edition, with custom art and layout.
[48 pages, perfect bound digest, Color cover, black & white interiors, MSRP $4.41]
[Available from lulu Print on Demand]



FIRST PARTY SCENARIOS

THE PARTHENOGENESIS OF HUNGRY HOLLOW
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Amanda Lee Franck
Small town coastal horror. Can you survive the flesh bees and strange residents in time to beacon in the town’s newest member?
[116 pages, 6" x 9" sewn hardcover , Color cover, black & white interiors, MSRP $40]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

THE MALL REMASTERED
Goblin Archives, Zach Hazard Vaupen, Garbage Goat, Trevor Henderson, Luke Sanders
The Mall is a wet, gooey, practical effects filled TTRPG adventure set in a 1990s mall pulled out of time and space. “What if The Thing were set in a ‘90s mall?” Winner of the Silver ENnie (2025) for Best Adventure - Short Form.
[40 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, B&W cover and interiors, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

THE BLOOM
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Amanda Lee Franck
Small town fungal horror in this sandbox scenario. “What if Twin Peaks had a The Last of Us season?” Nominated for a 2024 Ennie - Best Adventure Long Form.
[44 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, Color cover and interiors, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

CASE FILES
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Zach Hazard Vaupen
Liminal Horror’s official semi-annual anthology series. Each volume contains an assortment of strange and horrid creatures, supplemental mechanics, ready to run scenarios, and new character options. Currently on vol. 1.
[36 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, Color cover and interiors, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

EINFÜHLUNG
Goblin Archives, Josh Domanski, Zach Hazard Vaupen, Enoch Duncan
Einfühlung is an introductory scenario for Liminal Horror, providing nearly everything you need to jump in to play for both brand new and well-seasoned players. Every house hides secrets…some more sinister than others.
[44 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, Color cover and interiors, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

THIRD PARTY SCENARIOS
PLAYTHINGS
Seth Ian, Eric Hill, Tony Jaguar
Playthings is a Liminal Horror funnel where investigators wake to find themselves unknowingly trapped inside a Resonant Artifact which pulls those who activate it into a demi-plane of plastic, a world which caters not to the characters but to the needs and entertainment of another.
[24 pages, Half letter saddle stitched zine, Color cover and interior, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

EX INFERIS
Tyler Welch, BoRyan Crum
The winner of the Liminal Horror Twisted Classic, this scenario reimagines the cult sci-fi movie Event Horizon (1997) as a modern-day nautical nightmare. The ship never returned to port as rumors of a demonic ship crewed by Eyeless ghosts began to spread across the region.
[28 pages, A5 saddle stitched zine, Color cover, B&W interior, MSRP $15]
[Available from Space Penguin Ink, ACD]

IRON GODS
Tyler Welch
Anabolic Annihilation. Iron Gods is a body horror dungeon crawl set in a neon-soaked 1980s bodybuilding world. Professional wrestling x Cthulhu mythos / Body Snatchers x Pumping Iron.
[X pages, Glossy 8.5”x11” magazine size, Color cover, B&W interior, MSRP $15]
[Available from The Mighty Odd]


LIMINAL GRIMOIRE BOXSET
M. Allen Hall
A boxed collection of trifold pamphlet supplements for Liminal Horror. M. Allen Hall’s Alone in the Dark rules for solo play with Liminal Horror, solo rules summary, solo character sheets, and 6 stand-alone adventures (for solo play or group play) with an accompanying campaign guide.
[9 pamphlets + character sheets, 8.5" x 3.5" leaflets, 9" x 4" box, full color, MSRP $40]
[Available from M Allen Hall, Indie Press Revolution]

UHF CONTACT
M. Allen Hall
M. Allen Hall expands on his Liminal Horror solo ruleset to present three connected scenarios that are able to be run for a group, but can be used to facilitate solo or GM-less play with the included Alone in the Dark rules.
[70 pages, A5, section-sewn softcover, Color cover, B&W interior, MSRP $24]
[Available from M Allen Hall, ACD]

ELEVATOR GAME
M. Allen Hall
Someone has gone missing, and it seems to be connected to the punk zine Elevator Game. Three zines (included as player handouts) contain the clues, places to visit, and NPCs to help build out the world.
[40 pages, A5, staple-bound booklet + 3x 8-page, A6, staple-bound zines, MSRP $20]
[Available from M Allen Hall, ACD]

ON THE HILL
M. Allen Hall
A twisted retelling of B5: Horror on the Hill (TSR, 1983) that includes an 11-point Voidcrawl through Gareth Pines State Park with tables, Fallouts, Clues, and Horrors, along with 2 player-facing maps to be found during the adventure.
[16 pages, A5, staple-bound booklet, Color cover & interior, MSRP $14]
[Available from M Allen Hall]

WELCOME TO ST. JULIAN'S
Kyle Tam, Fabian Lelay, Johnny Isorena
A time-bending mystery set in a haunted school, rife with factions, multiple locations, divergent timelines, custom character creation and unique tables designed specifically for the story. Welcome to St. Julian’s Catholic School, where you can get the finest education…if you can survive long enough to graduate.
[20 pages, staple-bound booklet, Color cover & interior, MSRP $20]
[Available from Plus One EXP]

LAYERS OF UNREALITY
Josh Hittie
For those who loved The Backrooms. Explore the endless threads of the Skein in a procedurally driven liminal dungeon crawl compatible with Liminal Horror. Face the horrors and hauntings of reality’s backstage. Delve deep, become changed. Escape if you can.
[40 pages, staple-bound booklet, Color cover, B&W interior, MSRP $20]
[Available from Plus One EXP]

I DON’T BELONG HERE
Adam Stations, Strega Wolf Eden, M. Allen Hall
A Liminal Horror Radio Head inspired sourcebook that includes over 20 illustrated spark table spreads along with a bestiary, fallouts and resonant artifacts, spells, strange NPC and environments. It also includes a new Liminal Horror solo adventure by M. Allen Hall.
[98 page hardcover, US Trade (6x9"), full color, smyth-sewn binding, MSRP $30]
[Available from 72 Stations, Indie Press Revolution]

ENCOUNTER CARDS (I DON’T BELONG HERE)
Adam Stations
This unique set of tarot-sized Encounter Cards features full-art illustrations on the front, with an encounter description, stats and mini-tables on the back. These include art from: Trevor Henderson, Evangeline Gallagher, Zach Hazard Vaupen, Metafables, Anni Jyn, Laura Ketchum, Amanda Lee Franck, Vil, Strega Wolf Eden

[Available from 72 Stations]

A Liminal Horror hack/setting/rulebook for playing a space traveler-turned-necromancer on a quest to achieve eternal life by constructing a magical device known as an anchor.
