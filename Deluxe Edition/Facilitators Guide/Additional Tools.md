## **ADDITIONAL TOOLS**
There are many additional Facilitator tools that can help support your running of Liminal Horror.
1. Liminal Horror Deluxe Edition has hundreds of pages of Facilitator Tools and References.
2. [SRD v3.0](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd/) is a design aid document for Facilitators and creators to make content for Liminal Horror. It is specifically designed to support creation and has a multiude of different modular rules and writing advice. Some that may be of interest are:
  - [How to create Resonant Artifacts (including exemplars)](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd-resonant-artifacts/)
  - [How to create NPCs (including exemplars)](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd-npcs/)
  - [How to create Factions (including exemplars)](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd-factions/)
  - [How to create Cults (including exemplars)](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd-cults/)
  - [Modular Rules and Playstyles](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/srd-modular-rules/)
3. The Liminal Horror Library has a tag for [https://liminalhorrorlibrary.com/tag/facilitator-resources/].
4. Official Digital Generators:
  - Backgrounds: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-backgrounds](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-backgrounds)
  - Archetypes: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-archetypes](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-archetypes)
  - Cult Generator: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-cults](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-cults)
  - Legacy Spell Generator: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-spells](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-spells)
  - Rituals: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-rituals](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-rituals)
  - Liminal Horror NPCs Generic/Odd: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-generic-odd-npcs](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-generic-odd-npcs)
  - Investigator Generator: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-investigator-character-generator](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-investigator-character-generator)
  - Horror seeds: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-horror-seeds](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-horror-seeds)
  - Omens: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-omens](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-omens)
  - Location Generator and Stocking Procedure: [https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-location-generator-and-stocking-procedure](https://perchance.org/liminal-horror-location-generator-and-stocking-procedure
5. Third Party Digital Generators can be found under the Liminal Horror Library tag [Generators](https://liminalhorrorlibrary.com/tag/generators/).
