# Dinosaurs
A list of baseline dinosaur stats.


## Dinosaur
STR: 12 DEX: 12 CTRL: 8 HP: 6 Armor 1
Bite (d8) or tail-whip (d6+d6 blast)
- Roar: (d4 Stress blast)
- Big-game hunter: Will target the foe it deems most threatening first.
Rows of sharp teeth and a long segmented spiked tail. 5ft tall.


## Water Dinosaur 
STR: 12 DEX: 10 CTRL: 8 HP: 4 Armor 2
Bite (d4+d4) or tail (d8)
- Plated Scales: Provide protection
- Land and Water: Low to the grown (stubby legs) and long body. Prefers water but will come to land to grab prey.
- Finisher: If it causes Critical Damage it will bring its prey into the water and thrash it around below the surface, causing d6+d6 per subsequent round.
Crocodile-like dinosaur.


## Blob-like dino 
STR: 12 DEX: 10 CTRL: 8 HP: 4 Armor 2
Bite (d4) or crash land (d8)
- Jump: Uses surprisingly strong legs to launch its massive form, allowing it to leap up to 80 ft.
- Swallow-whole: If it causes Critical Damage it will swallow the victim whole. They will suffocate if not freed. Each jump while in its stomach causes d6 damage. It is slow to digest, letting the suffocated victim rot as a means of breaking it down.


## T-REX
STR: 18 DEX: 11 CTRL: 11 HP: 23 Armor 3
Bite (d10+d10), crush (d12) or tail-whip (d6 blast)


## Stegosaurus
STR: 16 DEX: 9 CTRL: 12 HP: 14 Armor 3
Tail-whip (d6+d6 blast)
- Trample: Its sheer size means that any creatures near it run the risk of being trampled. They must make a DEX save if near its feet or take (d6+d6 blast)


## Gecko-like dinosaur
STR: 12 DEX: 13 CTRL: 7 HP: 7 Armor 2
Bite (d6), pounce attack (d6+d6)
Able to climb surfaces (walls, trees, even ceilings) and pounce on targets.
5 feet long, large feet, primarily hunts at night.


## Chameleon-like dinosaur
STR: 12 DEX: 13 CTRL: 7 HP: 8 Armor 2
Bite (d6), tongue (d4+d4)
- Camouflage: able to change its skin to blend in with its surroundings. Uses this to make surprise attacks and to flee.
- Tongue: Is able to launch a long sticky tongue that can entangle prey and bring them close so it can bite.
Uses its tail to knock enemies prone.
7 foot long scaled dinosaur.


## Pterodactyl
STR: 12 DEX: 14 CTRL: 7 HP: 8 Armor 1
Claw (d6)
Flying dinosaur.
Will attempt to grab victims and bring them up into the air to drop them (making it easier to eat once they are broken open).
