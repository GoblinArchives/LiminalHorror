# **Echo**
**STR:** 12 **DEX:** 15 **CTRL:** 14  
**HP:** 10 **Armor:** 2  
Tentacle (d10, Blast)

- Echo of an Old Power.
- Quickly fading, must use flesh to maintain its frequency.
- Critical damage: Avatar rends the victim and absorbs it into itself (1d4 pieces). Fully heals STR.
