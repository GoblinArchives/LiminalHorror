# Adventure Conversions

Below are a collection of adventures made for a wide range of systems that could easily be converted for use with Liminal Horror. While some already include finished conversions, others are adventures with good bones that could be adapted. There is no right or wrong way to create a conversion guide, and in the end, the provided general and system specific advice can help Facilitators run things on the fly.

## Conversion Guide
The [Conversion Guide](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/conversion/) provides general advice on adapting adventures to Liminal Horror (from a wide range of genres and settings). It also includes [system specific conversion notes](https://liminalhorrorrpg.com/conversion/#specific-system-notes) that help give you a mechanics guide. Remember that these are just general notes (as opposed to steadfast edicts) and can be adjusted in order to prioritize interesting play and situations.

## Finished conversions
These can be used as an exemplar:
- [Fever Swamp](https://melsonian-arts-council.itch.io/fever-swamp) by Luke Gearing. Welcome to Fever Swamp, a fetid, forsaken place that no one in their right mind would enter. System: OD&D and Modern D&D Retro-Clones. [(LH Conversion)](https://unenthuser.itch.io/field-guide-to-fever-swamp)
- [Let Us In](https://wizardthieffighter.itch.io/let-us-in) by Luka Rejec. A Neo-70s modern slasher horror. In search for an inheritance, four heroes arrive to check up on rich relatives during a "harvest" festival. System: Agnostic. [(LH Conversion)](https://goblinarchives.itch.io/adventure-conversions-for-liminal-horror)

Yochai Gal and the Cairn community have some fantastic examples of conversion guides in practice over at [https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/conversions/](https://cairnrpg.com/adventures/conversions/) The system they use for presenting conversions is quite clear and streamlined.
